Yonghyun Bae

I am a Master's student at Xcelerating Lab (Ultrafast Electronic Structure Dynamics Lab) at Seoul National University, where I am advised by Prof. Ye-jin Kim.

My research interests lie in ultrafast electron dynamics in semiconductor materials. In particular, I aim to understand how quasiparticles such as excitons and polarons emerge from photoexcited carriers and evolve through relaxation and recombination, providing fundamental insights toward the development of more efficient semiconductor devices.
